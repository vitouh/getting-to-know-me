🌟 A Little About Me

🌆 Growing up in Tondo

I was born on February 13, 2014 in Tondo, Manila — a lively, colorful place that taught me how to be strong, kind, and hardworking. Even at 12, I know that my roots will always be my strength.

I love walking around our neighborhood, seeing everyday heroes, and dreaming about how I can help make things better when I grow up.

🏫 Manila Cathedral School

Grades 1–4. I was Class Vice President (Grade 3) and Class President (Grades 3–4). That’s where I learned to listen, lead, and care for my classmates. I also grew in my faith and love for learning.

Their motto stays with me: "Initium Sapientiae Timor Domini" (The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom).

⚙️ Don Bosco – My New Home

I transferred to Don Bosco Technical College, Mandaluyong in Grade 5 and plan to stay until Grade 6. The Bosconian spirit of Reason, Religion, and Kindness fits me perfectly.

I'm proud to be part of the Class of Sandor — a section full of energy and brotherhood.

📚 What I Love to Learn

Social Studies (Araling Panlipunan) is my favorite — especially history, political science, and law. I also love literature and writing. I believe that knowing the past helps us build a better future.

One day, I want to use words and wisdom to defend justice, teach young minds, or serve God as a priest — maybe all three!
