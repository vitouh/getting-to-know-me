Who Am I?

My name is Don Vito Jeron L. Reyes, a 12-year-old student with a heart full of ambitions and a mind drawn to the world of words, history, and justice. I grew up in Tondo, Manila — a place that shaped my resilience, my roots, and my love for my country.

I believe that knowledge is the most powerful tool a person can carry, and every chapter of my academic life has been a step toward becoming the person I am meant to be — a lawyer, a professor, or perhaps even a priest.
