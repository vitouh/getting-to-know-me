About Me 🙋

Hey! My name is Don Vito Jeron L. Reyes and I'm 12 years old. I grew up in Tondo, Manila, and I'm currently in Grade 5 at Don Bosco Technical College in Mandaluyong — Class of Sandor! 🙌
